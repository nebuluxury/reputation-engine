Social Media Ad Playbook

How we use paid + organic ads on Instagram and Facebook to recruit a salesforce, drive them to a landing page, and let them try the product before they sign up. Includes 10 ready-to-post ad creatives (5 English, 5 Spanish) with captions.

1. The whole idea in one line

We run cheap, punchy ads that promise real money for easy work ($20 per sale + $5/month residual), send clickers to a landing page where they can tap a live demo card themselves, and convert them into signed-up reps who order cards and start selling. The ad's only job is to get the click. The landing page's only job is to make them believe it works and sign up.

2. Who we target

  • Side-hustlers & gig workers - rideshare drivers, delivery, servers who want daytime income.
  • Students & young adults - comfortable on their phone, want flexible cash.
  • Direct-sales / MLM veterans - already know how to pitch, hate the inventory. This has none.
  • Bilingual communities - the Spanish creatives open up a huge, under-served recruiting pool (this is why we made both).

Paid targeting (Meta Ads): ages 18-45, interests in "side hustle / extra income / entrepreneurship / direct sales," plus a broad "advantage+" audience and let Meta optimize. Retarget anyone who visited the landing page but did not sign up.

6. How to A/B test (find the winner fast)

  1. Run 3-4 creatives at once, same budget, same audience, for 4-5 days.
  2. Kill the bottom half by cost-per-click and cost-per-signup. Do not judge by likes.
  3. Pour budget into the winner. Then make 2 new variations of the winner and repeat.
  4. Test hook angles: "money now" (ad 1, 5) vs "residual income" (ad 2) vs "no experience" (ad 3) vs "be your own boss" (ad 4). Different cities respond differently.

Honest note on ad claims

The income figures ($20/sale, $5/month, $60/day) are real per-sale mechanics, but "$60/day" assumes 3 sales a day, which not everyone will hit. Meta may flag ads that read as guaranteed income. Keep the wording as "earn up to" / "you can make," never "guaranteed," to stay inside Facebook/Instagram ad policy and avoid account bans.

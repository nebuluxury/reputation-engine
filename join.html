Questions

Good. Here are straight answers.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. Joining is free and you never pay for inventory - we provide the cards and ship them to you. You sell each card for $30, keep $20, and the remaining $10 covers the card and the platform. No membership fee, no "starter kit," no catch.

Do I need sales experience?

No. This is one of the simplest things to sell because the product demos itself in 30 seconds and it clearly helps the business. We give you a short script and the training. If you're friendly and consistent, you'll do well.

How and when do I get paid?

You keep your $20 margin on every card at the point of sale. The $5/month residual is paid out to you on a regular schedule for as long as the business stays subscribed (up to 12 months per business).

Is this a scam or MLM?

No. You don't recruit anyone and you don't buy inventory to "qualify." You make money one way: selling a real product that real businesses use. That's it.

How much time does it take?

Totally up to you. Some people do it a few hours a week for side income; others go full-time. The more shops you visit, the more you earn - the calculator above shows the range.

What areas can I work?

Any city, any neighborhood with local businesses. You choose where you sell. Spanish-speaking? Even better - there are huge under-served areas.
